# Orbit Store 1.3.0 (Beta)

**A new look for Orbit, with 400 more games to explore.**

- **400 new games:** 200 PS5 and 200 PS4, bringing Orbit’s catalogue to **1,601 games**: 1,001 PS5 and 600 PS4.
- **2,085 more download options.** There are 532 links for the new games and **1,553 additional source or format choices for 700 existing games**. The catalogue now offers **4,050 download options** across Archive.org, Vikingfile and AkiraBox. **Our promise remains the same: single-link downloads.**
- **Three new interfaces across all apps.** Choose **Crossbar**, **Cinema** or **Panorama** in the native PS5 app, browser and Orbit Zero. Store remains the default, and your queue, favourites and settings stay with you when you switch.

## Orbit Store · PS5 browser and native app

- **Browse without page breaks.** Keep scrolling through Discover’s All games, Browse and Library. More items appear as you move through the list.
- **Controller navigation throughout.** Move between sections, games and their actions, with menus and confirmation panels keeping the selection while they’re open.

## Orbit Zero · Mac, Windows and Linux

- **A refreshed desktop experience.** The new interfaces also cover Console, Downloads, Library and Settings.
- **More Browse controls.** Show favourites, filter by download size, change the sort order and reset your filters across every interface.
- **Find your downloaded files.** Download details show the full PS5 destination and the computer location while a cached copy remains.
